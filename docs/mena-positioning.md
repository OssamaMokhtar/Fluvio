# MENA Positioning — Slang

**Status:** Positioning document — for strategy and messaging.

## The Niche

Slang's defensible niche is **MENA**: Arabic pronunciation for English learners in the Gulf, or English pronunciation for Arabic speakers.

This is not a generic AI pronunciation coach competing with ELSA Speak. This is a bilingual, culturally-aware pronunciation tool built for the MENA market.

## Why MENA

### Market Factors

- **Arabic is one of the most common native languages among English learners in the Gulf.** Millions of Arabic speakers learning English for work, education, and migration.
- **English pronunciation for Arabic speakers has specific pain points.** Pharyngeals, emphatic consonants, vowel length, stress patterns — these are different from the pain points of Spanish or French speakers.
- **The MENA EdTech market is growing but under-served.** Global players (ELSA Speak, Duolingo, Babbel) are English-first. Regional players are few and often low-quality.
- **Bilingual AR/EN is a moat.** Global apps can't easily replicate bilingual, culturally-aware design. The portfolio's regional expertise is the differentiator.

### Product Factors

- **Arabic pronunciation for English learners:** Teach English sounds that don't exist in Arabic (p/b distinction, v/f, th, vowel length). Use Arabic phonology as the starting point.
- **English pronunciation for Arabic speakers:** Same product, different angle. Arabic speakers learning English.
- **Bilingual interface:** The app in Arabic and English. RTL support. Arabic reference audio. Arabic lesson plans.
- **GCC cultural context:** Prayer times, Ramadan fasting, weekend Fri–Sat — these affect when and how people practice.

## Messaging

**Don't say:** "AI pronunciation coach" (generic, competing with ELSA)

**Do say:** "Pronunciation practice for Arabic speakers learning English" or "English pronunciation coach for the MENA region"

**Value proposition:** "Pronunciation practice designed for Arabic speakers — not a generic app translated into Arabic, but built from the ground up for the MENA learner."
