# Privacy Policy

**Status:** Draft — for prototype phase. Not a substitute for legal advice.

## Data We Collect

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Voice recordings | Pronunciation analysis, phoneme scoring | Per user; deletable on request |
| Phoneme scores | Progress tracking, lesson plan generation | Per user; deletable on request |
| Lesson plan history | Practice tracking | Per user; deletable on request |
| LLM prompts/responses | Pronunciation analysis, TTS, lesson generation (server-side only) | Minimized; not persisted by default |

## Data Storage

- Voice recordings and scores are stored in the application's database (prototype: local development only).
- No data is shared with third parties except as required for LLM inference (Gemini API, server-side only).
- Voice recordings are biometric/PII data under UAE PDPL and handled accordingly.

## Your Rights

Under UAE PDPL and applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (including voice recordings)
- Withdraw consent (where processing is consent-based)

## Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the maintainer.
